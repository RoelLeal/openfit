# Code of Conduct

OpenFit is a small project maintained by volunteers. We want contributing to be
a pleasant experience for everyone, regardless of experience, background or
identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code and ideas, not people.
- Respect that maintainers' time is limited; a "no" to a feature is not personal.
- Remember that this app handles personal health data: never ask people to share
  their backups or logs in public.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, sexualised language, doxxing,
sustained disruption of discussions, or any conduct that would be inappropriate
in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for
contact details). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions and comments, and may temporarily or permanently ban
anyone who violates this code.

This document is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
